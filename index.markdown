## About the lab

We design **microfluidic platforms and engineered soft-matter objects** — functional emulsion droplets, degradable microcapsules, surface-functionalised particles — and use them as quantitative tools for biology. Controlling interface chemistry, mechanics and confinement at the microscale lets us ask precise questions about how immune cells sense and engulf targets, how single cells and microalgae behave under controlled conditions, and how plants and bacterial biofilms develop.

The team is hosted at the [Institut Pierre-Gilles de Gennes](https://www.institut-pgg.fr), a dedicated microfluidics institute with shared clean-room and microscopy facilities, and is part of the [CPCV laboratory](https://www.chimie.ens.fr/) (ENS-PSL, Sorbonne Université, CNRS). I also direct the [Microfluidics and Miniaturization Master](https://microfluidics-master.fr/) and co-founded [Quintessence Biotech](https://www.linkedin.com/company/quintessence-biotech/).
