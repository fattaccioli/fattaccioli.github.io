I am an **Associate Professor (HDR) in Chemistry** at Sorbonne Université and lead the **MesoBioLab** team within the [CPCV laboratory](https://cpcv-beta.vercel.app/) (UMR 8228 — ENS-PSL, Sorbonne Université, CNRS), hosted at the [Institut Pierre-Gilles de Gennes](https://www.institut-pgg.fr) in Paris. Trained as a physical chemist at ESPCI and in J. Bibette's group, I work at the interface of **microfluidics, soft matter, biophysics and immunology**, with a particular focus on **droplet-based microfluidic systems** and functional emulsions.

**Research.** We design microfluidic platforms and interface chemistries to generate and functionalise droplets, control microscale environments, and build quantitative experiments that connect physico-chemical principles to biological questions — notably in immunology, where droplets serve as artificial targets and micro-compartments for single-cell and high-throughput assays. [More on the research page](/research/).

**Teaching and training.** I teach Python for science, image analysis, microscopy and microfluidics at Bachelor and Master level, direct the [Microfluidics and Miniaturization Master](https://microfluidics-master.fr/), and run professional training in microfluidics for R&D teams. [More on the teaching page](/teaching/).

**Innovation.** I am co-founder and scientific advisor of **Quintessence Biotech**, a start-up born from the laboratory's patent portfolio that develops microfluidic and physico-chemical solutions for cell and gene therapies.
